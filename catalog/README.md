## Upaniṣad normalization

The supplied list contained 109 comma-separated labels because `Javali` and `Darshana` were separated. The catalogue records the Muktikā-style title as `Jabala-Darshana` and preserves the two supplied forms as aliases. Several other spellings remain flagged for authority-file and manuscript-catalogue review rather than being silently “corrected.”

## Meaning of coverage

`requested-scope` means the title was requested and has a catalogue record. It does not mean that:

- the work has a universally settled canonical status;
- the traditional author attribution is historically certain;
- all recensions have been identified;
- a digital edition is available;
- a host permits redistribution; or
- every commentary tradition has been mapped.

Commentary coverage is relational. A commentary is linked to its base text and school wherever known. This prevents one tradition's reading from being mistaken for the root text itself.
