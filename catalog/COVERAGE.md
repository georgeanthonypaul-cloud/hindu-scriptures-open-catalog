# Corpus Coverage

Coverage is measured per digital edition, not merely per ancient title. A Sanskrit root text, an English translation, a traditional commentary, and a modern critical apparatus are separate works with separate rights.

## Current verified mirror

The manifest contains 29 downloaded files with pinned SHA-256 checksums:

- 2 CC BY 4.0 Sanskrit corpora: Rāmāyaṇa and Mahābhārata.
- 15 CC BY-SA Sanskrit/TEI files: root texts, critical editions, and classical commentaries from SARIT.
- 10 public-domain-US English Project Gutenberg books, including the four-volume Ganguli Mahābhārata.
- 2 public-domain-US Max Müller Upaniṣad OCR volumes derived from 1879 and 1884 editions.

## Requested records with mirrored coverage

- Sanskrit and English: the Mahābhārata and its eighteen parvans; Bhagavad Gītā; Yoga Sūtras.
- Sanskrit: Rāmāyaṇa and its kāṇḍas; Brahma Purāṇa; Manusmṛti; Nāradasmṛti; Yājñavalkya Smṛti with Mitākṣarā; Caraka Saṃhitā; Suśruta Saṃhitā; Kauṭilya Arthaśāstra; Nyāya Sūtra with Vātsyāyana Bhāṣya.
- Public-domain English: twelve major Upaniṣads; Harivaṃśa; Viṣṇu Purāṇa; Brahma Sūtras with Śaṅkara and Rāmānuja commentarial traditions.
- Partial Sanskrit: the SARIT Skandapurāṇa critical-edition file covers the chapters identified in its TEI header, not the complete received Purāṇa.

## Outstanding scope

Of the 248 requested catalogue records, 195 remain link-only pending an exact reusable edition. The largest gaps are:

- complete Sanskrit and English pairs for all four Vedas, Brāhmaṇas, and Āraṇyakas;
- public-domain English translations for most of the 108-title Upaniṣad list;
- most Mahāpurāṇas and Upapurāṇas;
- many minor Smṛtis, Vedāṅgas, Āgamas, Tantras, and sectarian manuals;
- school-specific commentary chains beyond the initial Nyāya, Yoga, Vedānta, Mīmāṃsā, Vaiśeṣika, Dharmaśāstra, Purāṇa, and Ayurveda files.

An item remains link-only when a scan is merely readable online, when a modern translator or editor may still hold copyright, when the digital transcription has no reuse grant, or when completeness and recension cannot be established.
